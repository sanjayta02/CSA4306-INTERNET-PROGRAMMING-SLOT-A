PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy & demo data

This academic prototype stores demo accounts, bookings, comments, and schedules in your browser's local storage. The data is not securely transmitted to a server and may be visible to anyone using the same browser profile.

Do not enter real passwords, payment card information, or sensitive personal information. A production application would require a secure backend, password hashing, access controls, an approved privacy policy, and appropriate data-retention practices.
