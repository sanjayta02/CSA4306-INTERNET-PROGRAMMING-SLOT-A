ABOUT THE PROJECT

Train reservation, reimagined.

RailReserve is a responsive Internet Programming lab prototype demonstrating common online railway reservation workflows.

Project modules

  • Passenger registration, login and password recovery
  • Train search by origin, destination, date and class
  • Passenger details, fare summary and ticket generation
  • PNR lookup with Confirmed, RAC and Waiting List status labels
  • Booking history, ticket printing and cancellation/refund estimates
  • Admin schedule management and CSV report generation

This is an educational prototype. It does not connect to official train data, real seat inventory, payment gateways or email services.
