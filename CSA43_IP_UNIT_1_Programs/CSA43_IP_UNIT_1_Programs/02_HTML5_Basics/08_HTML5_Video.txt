PROGRAM 9: EMBED VIDEO IN HTML5
Aim: Embed a video file with browser playback controls.

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head><meta charset="UTF-8"><title>HTML5 Video</title></head>
<body>
    <h1>HTML5 Video Example</h1>
    <video width="480" height="270" controls>
        <source src="sample.mp4" type="video/mp4">
        <source src="sample.ogv" type="video/ogg">
        Your browser does not support the video element.
    </video>
    <p>Place sample.mp4 or sample.ogv in the same folder as this HTML file.</p>
</body>
</html>

Expected result: Video controls are displayed. Playback requires a supported video file.
